# Piano songs (MIDI)

centuries old (public domain); these MIDI files are the typeset editions of
the [Mutopia Project](https://www.mutopiaproject.org/), downloaded
2026-10-16. Seven are public domain; three are under
[CC BY-SA](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), credited below
(the files are unchanged, only renamed).
