# J111 · Pictures drawn, power buttons

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N2, N3
**From the user:** request `2026-10-27 #1` (reports N3VTFS, GADATU; "a cooler mode that caps the engine ... buttons for cooler and EXTREME")
**Verdict:** doable now

## Goal
The bar answers your touch quickly: Ultra and Auto keep the screen getting
a new picture every refresh, not just the game's clock ticking. Settings →
Graphics gets Power: Cooler (30 a second, a cooler phone), Balanced, EXTREME
(full sharpness always, no frame limit).

## Research
- GADATU's GPU check on Ultra: 63 of 121 frames drew a picture with
  everything on; 119 with 6 lamps a pixel; 121 at 1x pixels; 40 with one
  frame queued (worse). Touch to screen 46–85 ms, worst 130. No frame was
  "late": while the GPU is busy the game skips drawing (`looped/gpu.cpp`
  mayDraw) and the clock still ticks at 60, so J110's late-frame watching
  never saw it.
- The lamps: the light grid was 16 x 16 over the whole floor (cells ~1.5 m);
  a pixel goes through every lamp listed for its cell, and the cap (16 Ultra
  … 8 Low) shortens every lamp's reach until no cell holds more.
- `maxFps` (looped/gpu.h) already caps drawing (tests use ?fps=); the logic
  runs every frame either way.

## Pieces
- **J111.1** A finer light grid: 48 x 48 cells (~0.5 m): Ultra pixels go
  through fewer lamps (8.0 → 6.8 on average) for the same picture; at High
  and below lamps reach further (High: 3.55 → 2.27 lux). *Check:* the bar
  looks the same on Ultra; a bit brighter at a distance on High.
- **J111.2** Ultra and Auto watch pictures drawn: under 90 % drawn is
  "behind"; Ultra eases 3x → 2.5x → 2x, then 12 and 10 lamps a pixel; back
  up when steady. *Check:* on Ultra a report shows nearly every frame drawn.
- **J111.3** Power: Cooler / Balanced / EXTREME in Settings → Graphics.
  Cooler draws 30 a second (and eases against that); EXTREME never eases
  and has no frame limit. *Check:* Cooler: the phone stays cooler, motion a
  bit less smooth; EXTREME: always the sharpest.
