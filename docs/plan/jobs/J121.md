# J121 · A world the size of Skyrim, made by our own rules, in the best light a phone can show (ENG)

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N2 (made from scratch), N3 (real light)
**From the user:** requests `2026-11-02 #3` ("the most realistic graphics with nanite … world gen for a real map like something as big as Skyrim") and `#4` ("a I want it to be ours fully": an invented world, no outside map data)
**Verdict:** needs an experiment: a streamed 6 × 6 km world generated from our own rules, with Looped's clusters for what stands on it, is known practice; how far realism goes on an iPhone in a browser (memory, heat) is found step by step, each step judged on the phone

## Goal
A world as big as Skyrim's (about 6 × 6 km) that you walk across on your
phone: mountains, valleys, rivers, forests, villages, a sun and sky, made
from our own rules in Looped Script, loaded around you as you go; the story
spread across it.

## Pieces (each one playable, in Barcode162/ENG)
- **J121.1** The land: an island 6 km across from our own noise (hills, ridged mountains, beaches, snow), drawn near-to-far by a quadtree (fine near you, coarse far away), walked on; a place in Looped Script says `the land: seed 7, 6 km across`. *Check:* walk to a mountain on the phone.
- **J121.2** Forests and rocks on the land, drawn with the clusters (our Nanite). *Check:* a forest to the horizon, smooth.
- **J121.3** The sun: a time of day, shadows, a sky that scatters light. *Check:* sunset over the mountains.
- **J121.4** Water: the sea, rivers and lakes carved into the land. *Check:* follow a river to the sea.
- **J121.5** Villages and roads, by rules. *Check:* walk a road between two villages.
- **J121.6** The Last Ember spread across the world.

## Done when
Each piece: native tests pass, headless pictures on WebGPU and WebGL2, the
user's phone check.

## Risks
Phone memory and heat: each piece has a quality level and an off switch.
