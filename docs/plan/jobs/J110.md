# J110 · Smoother graphics

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N2, N3
**From the user:** request `2026-10-26 #14` (report SC7QKJ, "Fix it first"), after #13 (report YYXLV8: "how bad it's going for my cpu")

## Goal
The bar stutters less on the user's iPhone, at the quality they pick, with
the same picture. The GPU check and the reports show what you feel (late
frames), not a number that only looks bad.

## Research
- **What the reports measured.** `looped/profiler.cpp` `trackGpu` times
  from a frame's submit to `OnSubmittedWorkDone`'s callback. On Safari that
  callback comes through the page's event loop after the frames queued
  ahead of it, so "GPU 58 ms" (SC7QKJ) is a wait of about 3–4 frames, not
  the chip's working time: the same report's frames came every 17.4 ms on
  average (about 57 fps), with 28 of 300 frames late (21–42 ms) and the CPU
  at 1.3 ms. The GPU check's steps all read 16.7 ms a frame (vsync caps it),
  so only their waits differed: 58 everything; 52 no soft shade; 50 no
  beams; 54 no lamp shadows; 39 no bounced light; 31 six lamps a pixel; 13
  1x pixels. The order of costs holds (pixels, lamps a pixel, bounced light);
  the sizes don't.
- **Auto** (`looped/quality.h`) steps down when frames are slow, the CPU
  small and "GPU" over 12 ms, and up only when "GPU" × the pixel ratio² is
  under 11 ms: with a wait of 3–4 frames that is never true, so Auto never
  climbs back.
- **The lamp loop** (`looped/shaders/scene.wgsl` `shade`): each pixel reads
  its floor cell's list (up to 16 lamps, brightest first); a lamp that
  doesn't reach or faces away is skipped; a shadowed lamp takes 5 filtered
  depth looks (`lampShadow`); an unshadowed one asks the 8 probes around
  (`giLampVisible`). `giSample` keeps all 8 probes' lamp masks (32 numbers)
  plus 8 weights alive for the whole loop: on phone GPUs a shader that keeps
  many numbers alive runs fewer pixels at once.
- **How engines handle it:** dynamic resolution (Unreal, Unity, consoles)
  eases the 3D picture's pixels to hold the frame rate and keeps the
  sharpest the frame budget allows; clustered/tiled lighting skips lights
  that don't reach a pixel (we have a 2D grid); a light's contribution under
  what the screen can show is cut.
- **Risks:** a shader change can shift the picture (checked with pictures
  before/after at the same spots); dynamic sharpness can pump (hysteresis:
  steps only after 2 s windows, back up after 6 calm seconds, a bounce
  blocks climbing for a minute, as Auto already does).

## Pieces
- **J110.1** The GPU check and reports measure what you feel: each step's
  late frames (over 20 ms) and slowest 5 %, beside the wait. *Check:*
  Settings → Help Claude fix lag → Check the GPU, then send a report.
- **J110.2** Lighter lighting, the same picture: bounced light keeps fewer
  numbers per pixel; lamps too faint to see here are skipped, shadow looks
  included. *Check:* the bar looks the same; fewer stutters.
- **J110.3** Ultra and Auto keep it smooth: both step by late frames, not
  the wait; Ultra eases its sharpness (3x → 2.5x → 2x) only while frames run
  late and goes back to 3x when steady; Auto can climb back up again.
  *Check:* on Ultra, walk around: smooth; the log says when it eases.
