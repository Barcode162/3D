# J84 · Safety on the server, step 1: voice only between signed-in people of the same age group

**Chapter:** the backbone
**Serves:** N4
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #5` (build the fixes from Claude's review, safety first); the rule "13+ only; teens (13-17) and adults kept apart" (2026-10-19)
**Verdict:** doable now — the room already asks the accounts server who each login is and carries every voice handshake, so it can refuse the ones the rule forbids; only the age group travels (teen or adult), never the birth date

## Goal
Voice chat only ever connects two signed-in people of the same age group:
teens with teens, adults with adults. A guest, or an account made before
birth dates were asked, can't start or receive a voice call; the bar says
why and how to fix it (sign in; add your birth date once).

## Where it stands (Claude's review, 2026-10-25)
The bar is open to guests (gate.php: "Come in as a guest") and voice goes
phone to phone (WebRTC) with anyone in the room: no age is known for a
guest, nothing keeps teens and adults apart, and each side sees the other's
internet address. The Charter's rule says teens and adults are kept apart.

## Scope
The room (cloud/room.js) learns each login's age group with the login check
it already makes (account.php's own profile carries `band`), keeps it with
the player (and through hibernation), and forwards a voice handshake
(SIGNAL) only when both players have a login and the same known group.
A refused handshake goes back to its sender as `{refused: "guest" | "birth" |
"group"}`. The bar's voice panel says it in words. account.php gains one
action: an account without a birth date sets it once (13 and up, never
changed after).
**Not in scope:** text messages and lobbies by age group (J20), reports,
block and mute (J20), relays that hide addresses (J63, parked: a cost), the
casino (J22).

## Design
- `who()` in the room: `band` from `j.me.band` ("teen" | "adult" | "");
  test logins (DEV) say theirs (`badge` list `teen`).
- `onSignal(slot, body)`: both sides must have `acct` and the same non-empty
  band; else the sender gets `G {from: to, data: {refused: why}}` (the
  protocol's G already carries any data).
- bar/web/voice.js: a refused peer is remembered (no retries), and the voice
  panel shows "Voice is for signed-in players" (guest), "Add your birth date
  to talk" (no birth date, with the date field), or nothing for a group
  apart (you simply don't hear each other, and the panel says voice joins
  people of your own age group).
- account.php `{"a":"birth","token","birth"}`: only when none is set; the
  same checks as sign-up (a real date, 13 and up).

## Pieces
- **J84.1** The room refuses voice handshakes the rule forbids (guest,
  no birth date, teen with adult) and tells the sender why; the room test
  covers each case. *Check:* two phones, one signed in and one a guest: the
  guest's voice panel says voice is for signed-in players, and no one hears
  the guest
- **J84.2** The bar says why, and an older account adds its birth date
  once (account.php's new action, the voice panel's words and date field).
  *Check:* Menu → Voice, on an account made before birth dates: "Add your
  birth date to talk"; after adding it, voice works with other adults

## Done when
The room test passes the four cases (guest refused, no birth date refused,
teen with adult refused, adult with adult connects); the accounts tests
cover the birth action; live, a guest can't talk to anyone.

## Risks
The owner's own account may have no birth date: voice stops for it until
it's added once (the panel asks). A guest who signs in gets voice at once.
