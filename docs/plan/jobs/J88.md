**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N3 N5
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #5` (the engine a real one; the fixes from Claude's review)
**Verdict:** doable now — the renderer's own code; each part has an off switch, and the phone judges the cost (J37's report)

## Goal
Edges are smooth (no jagged neon, glass or table edges); every lamp keeps
its real reach (no light that changes because a part of the floor held too
many lamps); bounced light stops working the phone when nothing changes.

## Where it stands (Claude's review, 2026-10-25)
No edge smoothing at all; lamps sorted on a 16 x 16 floor grid with at
most 16 per square, and when a square is too full every lamp's reach is
shortened until it fits (the smoke test: "lamps reach down to 0.75 lux");
the bounced light does a bounce and refills its probes every frame.

## Design
- **Smooth edges:** 4x multisampling (MSAA) for the main view, resolved into
  the picture the glow and eyes read; cheap on the iPhone's GPU (it resolves
  on the chip). Off with `?msaa=0` and in Settings → Graphics.
- **Lamps in 3D:** the place's volume cut into 3D cells, each holding a
  32-bit list of the lamps that reach it (every lamp fits: at most 32), so
  no lamp is cut short; the shader walks the cell's lamps. `?lights=floor`
  keeps the old grid for comparison.
- **Bounced light at its own pace:** a bounce only while some lamp is
  changing (flicker, dimmers, a switch), at most 30 times a second; still
  light settles and stops costing.

## Risks
MSAA costs memory (the main view's samples): at Low quality it's off. More
lamps per pixel where many overlap: the phone report decides the default.
