# J88 · A real engine, step 3: smooth edges, lamps sorted in 3D, bounced light at its own pace

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N3 N5
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #5` (the engine a real one; the fixes from Claude's review)
**Verdict:** doable now — the renderer's own code; each part has an off switch, and the phone judges the cost (J37's report)

## Goal
Edges are smooth (no jagged neon, glass or table edges); every lamp keeps
its real reach (no light that changes because a part of the floor held too
many lamps); bounced light stops working the phone when nothing changes.

## Where it stands (Claude's review, 2026-10-25)
No edge smoothing at all; lamps sorted on a 16 x 16 floor grid with at
most 16 per square, and when a square is too full every lamp's reach is
shortened until it fits (the smoke test: "lamps reach down to 0.75 lux");
the bounced light does a bounce and refills its probes every frame.

## Research (2026-10-25, rule "Research, then build")
**What it touches:** the main pass in `SceneRenderer::render` (pipelines
`pipeline_`, `modelPipeline_`, `skinnedPipeline_`, `modelBlendPipeline_`,
the floor shadows `looped/shadows.cpp`, the mirror glass), the depth buffer
(`looped/gpu.cpp`), the post pass (`looped/post.cpp`: it draws the name
pictures and text **using the scene's depth**), the light list and floor
grid (`buildLighting`, `LightingUniforms`, `scene.wgsl`), and bounced light
(`looped/gi.*`, `gi.wgsl`). The mirrors and the reflection capture use only
the *mirrored* pipelines.
**Limits found:**
- **32 lamps at most** (`kMaxLights`), and the bar already has **29**. The
  world remake gives every bulb, sconce, neon sign and the fire its own light
  (J76): the limit must go up first.
- Bounced light remembers which lamps each probe sees in **one 32-bit mask**
  (`maskTex`, r32uint): also 32 lamps at most.
- The light grid is on the floor (16×16 cells, 1.53 m in the bar), at most
  16 lamps per cell and 12 per pixel; crowded cells shorten every lamp's
  reach (smoke log: "lamps reach down to 0.75 lux").
- Bounced light runs every frame (`gi_.encode` each frame), but flickering
  lamps (candles, the fire, neon) change every frame too: "only while lamps
  change" would still be every frame.
**MSAA on the phone:** Google's Filament rates 4x MSAA "high" cost on
mobile. A 4x picture in half-floats plus its depth is about 75 MB at the
phone's 860×1864. Chrome now has memory-saving "transient" attachments; Safari's
support isn't confirmed. Store ops "discard" on the 4x textures let the
phone's tile GPU skip writing them back.
**Traps found:**
- The post pass draws the name pictures with the scene's depth: with a 4x
  depth buffer the post pass can't use it directly. A tiny full-screen pass
  copies sample 0 of the 4x depth into a 1x depth (`texture_depth_multisampled_2d`).
- Only the main pass's pipelines become 4x; the mirrors and the capture stay
  1x (their pipelines are separate already).

## Design
- **Smooth edges: not in this job** (the user, request 2026-10-25 #13: MSAA
  costs speed instead of saving it; parked for later). The research above
  stays for when it comes back.
- **More lamps:** up to 255 lamps (still a uniform buffer: 25 KB of 64);
  bounced light keeps track of the first 127.
- **Lamps in 3D cells:** the place's volume cut into 3D cells (x, y, z), each
  listing the lamps that reach it at their full reach; the shader walks the
  cell's list. `?lights=floor` keeps the old grid for comparison.
- **Bounced light for 127 lamps:** the probes' lamp mask in 4 × 32 bits.
- **Bounced light at its own pace:** the slow parts (the bounce between
  surfaces) at most 10 times a second and only when the lamps' light really
  changed (more than 2 %); flicker is kept by the direct light, which is
  instant.

  Looked at before saving: the bar in real light, bounced light ready in

- **J88.2** ~~Lamps sorted in 3D cells~~: **left out after research**
  (2026-10-25). A simulation of the bar's 29 lamps: at 0.1 lux every lamp
  reaches every cell whatever the cells (16×16 floor: 29; 32×32: 28; 16×16×4
  in 3D: 29); lamps are shortened by the per-pixel cap (12 at High), not by
  the cells. 3D cells would cost work and change nothing. What would: more
  lamps per pixel (slower), or for the world remake many small bulbs drawn
  as one light (J76's design question).
