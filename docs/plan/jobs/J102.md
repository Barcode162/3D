# J102 · A real fire

**Chapter:** the world
**Serves:** N3
**From the user:** request `2026-10-26 #8` ("the fire shouldn't look like it's a couple moving images")
**Verdict:** doable now — a shader effect inside the fireplace; small on screen, cheap on the phone

## Goal
The fireplace's flames look like fire from every side: tongues of different
heights licking up along the logs, red at the edges, yellow in the heat,
never the same twice; tapped out, they sink to embers.

## Research (2026-10-26)
**What it touches:** `fire_flames.glb` (four crossed cards with a flame
picture, `tools/assets/lounge.py` build_flames), its kind (`fire_flames`:
loops burn/embers on scale.y and glow, the tap to put it out), the model
shader (`looped/shaders/scene.wgsl`, fs_model_blend), the model material
(`looped/gltf.*`, `looped/model.cpp`), the frame uniforms.
**Findings:**
- Cards look like pictures as soon as you see them edge-on or watch them
  move. Games draw close-up fire either as many small particles or as a
  volume the shader fills with noise (ray marching a box: Shadertoy-style
  "volumetric fire"); for one fireplace seen up close, the volume is
  simpler and looks solid from every angle.
- The engine had no time in its shaders and no way for a model to say "I'm
  a fire": a frame clock and a material flag (glTF extras "looped_flame")
  add both without touching other models.
- Cost: only the pixels of the firebox, 24 steps each, 4 noise octaves:
  small next to the frame.

## Design
- The flame volume: a box (half depth 0.17, height 0.6, half width 0.36 m)
  whose material's base colour carries its size; the shader marches the line
  of sight through it, with flames from rising noise, a height per stretch of
  logs, red-orange-yellow by heat, see-through where thin.
- The kind's loops keep working: glow scales the fire (embers), scale.y
  shrinks it; the thing now stands at the flames' base (no pivot), its tap
  area moved with it.

## Pieces
- **J102.1** The fire drawn by a shader. *Check:* stand by the fire: flames
  move and change like a real fire, no flat pictures; tap it out: embers

## Done when
The fire looks real on the phone, from the sofa and from the side.
