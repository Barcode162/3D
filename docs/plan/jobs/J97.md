# J97 · Lamp shadows

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N3
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #17` (reflections "through items", "patches of light with no lights making them"), `2026-10-26 #1` ("Real lamp shadows next"; their screenshot of build `007912d`: the lamps' highlights on the floor since the first version)
**Verdict:** doable at a cost — phone speed: each shadowed lamp adds a texture look-up per pixel; the user chose it; quality setting and an off switch

## Goal
Stools, tables, the counter and the walls block a lamp's light and its
highlight: no light or reflection on the floor where something stands
between it and the lamp.

## Research (2026-10-26, rule "Research, then build")
**What it touches:** `looped/shaders/scene.wgsl` (the lamp loop in `shade`:
today a lamp is hidden only where bounced light's probes, 0.2 m apart,
can't see it: walls, not stool legs), `looped/scene_renderer.*` (a depth
pass per lamp), `looped/render_scene.h` / `looped/world/place_layout.*`
(which model a lamp belongs to), the mirrors and the reflection capture
(they shade with the same code), Settings.
**How it works now:** no lamp has a shadow (KI-38). That's why the user's
first screenshot already shows the pendant lamps' highlights on the floor
under the stools.
**How engines do it:** for lamps that don't move, the shadows of things that
don't move are drawn once and kept (Unreal's "stationary" lights, Unity's
baked/mixed shadows); a point lamp's shadow is a cube (6 views). Moving
things (people) keep their own cheap shadows (here: the soft discs).
**Findings and traps:**
- The bar has 29 lamps; many are small bulbs (string lights, candles):
  shadows for the **16 brightest** at first, the rest as now.
- Drawn once, not every frame: 16 lamps × 6 faces × ~100 grouped draws
  (J87), spread over ~16 frames after loading; again when the Studio moves
  something (bounced light already knows when: `layoutChanged`).
- **A lamp sits inside its own model** (the bulb's glass, the shade): its
  own model must be left out of its shadow, or the lamp lights nothing.
  The layout knows which model a lamp's thing has; it travels with the lamp.
- Memory: 16 lamps × 6 × 256 × 256 depth (16-bit) = 12.6 MB.
- The lamp's highlight uses the same shadow (the user's complaint is the
  highlight).
- Edges: a few filtered look-ups (PCF), softer for bigger bulbs.
- Cube arrays are core WebGPU (Safari included); the shadow test only in
  the main view and mirrors, not in the reflection capture (cheap).
- Incidents that apply: judge the before/after region by region
  (2026-10-25-square-of-light), search the whole repo for every name that
  changes (2026-10-25-astc-label-renamed).

## Design
- `PointLight` gets the model it belongs to; `SceneRenderer` picks the 16
  brightest lamps, gives each a slice of a cube-array depth texture, and
  draws the still models into it (its own model left out), a few faces per
  frame, again after `layoutChanged`.
- `scene.wgsl`: a shadowed lamp no longer uses bounced light's probe test
  (its 0.2 m blocks are the squares of light, J98.1, request 2026-10-26 #2);
  in the lamp loop, a shadowed lamp's light (diffuse and
  highlight) times its cube look-up (4 taps, compared, softened by the
  bulb's size); the probes' wall test stays for the others.
- Settings → Graphics → Lamp shadows (on / off); `?shadows=0`.

## Risks
- A lamp that seems to light nothing: its own model wasn't left out.
- Acne (speckled shadows on lit surfaces): a depth bias, checked in the pictures.
- Moving things (glasses, darts) don't cast lamp shadows yet.
