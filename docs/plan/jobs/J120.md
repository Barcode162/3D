# J120 · The Last Ember: a story game on Looped, written in Looped Script (the ENG repo)

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N2 (made from scratch: our engine, our language)
**From the user:** requests `2026-11-02 #1` (a story game in the ENG repo, with everything a story needs, to see what the language and the engine can make) and `#2` (the language already exists: use it; ENG's own GitHub page; adventure / fantasy)
**Verdict:** doable now: Looped's new drawing piece (looped/draw, WebGPU and WebGL2) and Looped Script (looped/words) exist; the game copies them into Barcode162/ENG unchanged and adds the story words on top, from scratch in C++ (no outside engine or asset); its page is ENG's GitHub page, which the user chose (public, apart from the bar)

## Goal
An adventure you play on the phone from ENG's GitHub page: walk through
five places at night with a lantern, talk to the people there, choose what
to say and do, carry things, and reach one of several endings. The whole
story, its places and its people are written in Looped Script.

## Scope
In Barcode162/ENG: a copy of the engine pieces it uses (`looped/`, unchanged,
with the 3D commit it came from), the story words (reader, checker with line
and hint, runner), the game app (drawing, walking, the lights from the
language's light words), the page (thumbs, talk, choices, what you carry,
saving), the story itself, native tests, and a GitHub workflow that tests,
builds and publishes the page. **Not in scope:** any change to the bar, its
engine files or its deploys in Barcode162/3D; sound; outside models.

## Design
- The story language reads with the engine's own reader (`readLines`,
  problems with the line, plain words and "did you mean"); prose after a
  speaker's colon is kept as written.
- Lights are the engine's light words (`a flame that flickers`), worked
  out each frame by the engine's `Words::lights`.
- Shapes are drawn from boxes, posts, cones, balls and roofs made in code
  (no outside assets), one buffer per place, lit by up to 16 lights.
