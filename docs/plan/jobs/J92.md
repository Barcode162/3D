# J92 · No artificial glow

**Chapter:** the engine
**Serves:** N3 N5
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #10` ("get rid of the artificial glow and let only realistic light")
**Verdict:** doable now — removing a pass from our own renderer; it also saves the phone five passes per frame

## Goal
No haze of light painted around lamps, bulbs, neon and the fire: they are
bright themselves and light what's around them, the way real light does.

## Where it stands
After the scene, `looped/post.*` picked out everything brighter than
white, blurred it at 1/4, 1/8 and 1/16 of the screen and added it back
(bloom; the bar's mood: 0.7). It was off only at Low quality or `?post=0`.

## Design
- The bloom's three shaders, five passes and three textures go;
  the composite only runs the eyes, the AgX curve and the mood.
- The moods' `glow`, `glowThreshold`, `glowKnee`, the bar's settings, the
  `?glow=`, `?glowth=` and `?post=` page options go.
- Not touched: a thing's own brightness (neon, bulbs, the jukebox's light
  loops: real self-lit surfaces), the moths and sparks (small glowing
  things), the racing project's own lights.
- J44 loses "glare and starbursts" (the same trick).

## Pieces
- **J92.1** The glow taken out of the engine. *Check:* by the neon
  signs, the lamps and the fire: no haze of light around them, the bulbs
  and the neon still bright, the room still lit

## Done when
The quick checks and the bar's pictures pass; the pictures show the
haze gone and the rooms otherwise the same.

## Risks
Bulbs may look smaller and harder without the haze: that's how real
light looks; if a lamp now looks dull, its own brightness is raised, never
the glow brought back.
