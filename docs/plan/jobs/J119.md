## Goal
Everything today's bar does runs on the new engine; then, only when the
user says switch, joshuavelasco.com/bar opens the new engine for everyone
on every browser, and the old renderer, lighting and hand-kept JSON places
are retired.

## Where it starts
- The new engine: `labs/next/` (the app), `labs/web/next/` (its page, opened
  by `/bar/?engine=next`), the bake (`node tools/labs.mjs bar --bake`,
  about 10 minutes), the lights (`assets/world/rigs/bar.looped`, played each frame).
- The bar's world: `assets/world/places/bar.looped` (the source), made into
  `bar.json` by `node tools/words.mjs place bar`.
