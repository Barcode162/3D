## Goal
A new place, **the showroom**, reached from Menu → Places, where the user can
see on their phone that the engine works: one room with five stations, each
with a floating sign that says what it shows and what to tap.

## Scope
`assets/world/places/showroom.json` and `assets/world/kinds/showroom.json`
(new), published to the world store after the deploy so the Places list and
the room server know it. **Not in scope:** engine or page code, the bar, the
test home, the Places menu's own code (it lists every place the live world
has already).

## Design
- A 10 m square room of plain boxes (like the test home), 12 lights (the
  limit is 32), the bar's kinds reused where they exist.
- **Motion:** a big credit coin over a pedestal turns, bobs and shines on the
  shared clock; eight sparks circle it (orbit); tap the coin and it flips
  with a hop (a state with a timed rule).
- **Colour temperature:** four pendant lamps along the pale left wall, the
  same bulb at 1800 K, 2700 K, 4000 K and 6500 K over white cubes; tap a
  lamp and it swings.
- **Materials:** all 13 materials as cubes on one shelf under a long tube
  light (a light with a shape, L1.6); look at a cube to read its name.
- **Music:** the jukebox, three ceiling lights that pulse on the beat while a
  song plays, three neon tubes that breathe one after another.
- **Touch and physics:** a table that wobbles, a candle you blow out, glasses
  you pick up and throw, two stools to sit on, a chalkboard you knock.
- **The light switch** by the door dims the lamps, the shelf and the ceiling;
  the neon and the jukebox stay, so the eyes' adjustment shows.
- Signs are tap areas with a name and a prompt and no action ("none").
- The room server runs a place named in the live world for rooms called
  `place-<id>` (S6.4), so the store push is what makes taps and glasses
  shared there.
