## Goal
The bar on the new engine is the whole bar: written in Looped Script's
words, its lights alive (flickering, dimming when the lights go down, the
strings dancing to the music), looking as the user likes on their phone at
every quality level; then, when the user says switch, it becomes the bar
everyone walks into, on every browser.

## Scope

**Not in scope:** the bar's games and people on the new engine (they come

## Design
- **The bar in words:** a converter turns `assets/world/places/bar.json`
  into `bar.looped` (things placed by `at x y z, turned n` where no place
  word fits), with a test that the world made from the words equals
  today's thing by thing; then the words file is the source and the JSON
  is made from it (`node tools/words.mjs place bar`), as the lab's is.
- **Lights alive:** the bar's rig (`assets/world/rigs/bar.looped`) runs in
  the new engine each frame (looped/words/rig.h) and sets the ten channels'
  brightness; the page sends it the events (lights down, music).
- **Tune:** quality levels as the clusters' error limit and the light
  texture's size; smooth shading on round things; small things lit right
  (corner light measured against the reference); the bounce repaired
  after things move; the look matched to the user's liked screenshot
  (less warm than the first preview).
- **The switch:** everything today's bar does (people, seats, the games,
  the menus) running on the new engine, then `?engine=next` becomes the
  default and the old renderer, lighting and JSON world files are retired.

## Findings
- **J118.1:** the bar's words are a list place (`looped/words/place_list.h`):
  one line a thing, numbers kept as written, so the words make back the
  world file exactly (checked thing by thing, 603 things). An id is
  `<kind>-<n>` by its place among its kind, so new things go after the last
  of their kind (the recipes say so).
- **J118.2:** the rig runs in the new engine (no new bake: the baker lists
  the lamps by name, `node tools/bake.mjs lamps bar`); the page sends Lights
  down and Music. Today's bar plays its lights from the world's loops, as
  before.
- **J118.3:** the baked file is LNX2: each corner's facing (smooth round
  things), and packed for gzip (byte planes; indices as steps): 17.2 → 13.3 MB
  with the facings added. Quality: Low / Medium / High (pixels drawn and far
  detail). Colour: Warm (the first preview), Soft (default: about half the
  bulbs' orange taken out), Neutral.
