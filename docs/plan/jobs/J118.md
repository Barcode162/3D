# J118 · 4K Mania: taps don't stretch frames

**Chapter:** the world

## Goal
A tap never makes the picture skip a frame in 4K Mania, and the next report
says exactly where a late touch was held up.

## Research (report JJXXTD, build 285c547, iPhone, Safari 26.3)
- The game itself is light: about 1 ms of work a frame (CPU 0.5 ms, GPU 2.4 ms), 60 frames a second.
- But frames stretch to 21-39 ms exactly when fingers land (717 hitches; every recent one had a touch in it, with the game's own work under 1 ms).
- Touches reached the game 44 ms after the finger on average (2-3 frames), 560 of 1148 over 34 ms, once 920 ms (the "half-second freeze").
- Up to 5 fingers at once, 1 touch taken away by the phone. The gesture guard was on for the first 54 s and off after, so the numbers mix both.
- Per-tap work on the page outside the game: the report's tap recorder (`bar/web/index.html`, a capturing `pointerup` listener) calls `document.elementFromPoint` and `getBoundingClientRect` on every tap, which make the browser lay the page out again mid-frame; with the guard off, each tap also sends pretend mouse events whose handler (Emscripten's) measures the canvas again.

## Pieces
