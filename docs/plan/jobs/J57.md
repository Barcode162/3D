# J57 · The Loop Maker, step 2: the Studio's timeline, like a DAW or a video editor (formerly T2)

**Chapter:** the tools
**Serves:** N2 N3
**From the user:** request `2026-10-25 #5` ("a loop maker which get placed as animations and then the world comes to life … sort of like a video editor or DAW"); the Studio plan's Loop Rack (2026-10-21, "your FL Studio idea, for things")
**Verdict:** doable after J85 — the engine plays patterns and timelines first; the clips need no script language (events placed on the timeline still wait for J79)

## Goal
In the Studio, the place's timeline sits along the bottom like a music app's
arrangement or a video editor's tracks: a ruler in bars and beats, a
playhead, play, pause, scrub and a loop region. Each track is a thing or a
kind; the loop library's patterns are dragged onto a track as clips, moved,
stretched, faded, copied, deleted. Tap a clip to open its pattern in the
Loop Maker: a lane per channel (move, turn, size, glow, light, colour),
keys drawn with a finger, shapes and presets, saved back to the library.
The bar plays every edit at once; Publish gives it to everyone.

## Scope
studio/: a timeline module (the strip, tracks, clips, transport), the Loop
Maker (the Loop Rack grown into the pattern editor of the library), the
draft (the place's `timeline`, `loops.json`), the Changes list in words
("Lamps track: swell clip at beat 4, 8 beats long"), the Handbook's Studio
page. bar/studio_mode.cpp: the commands that swap the timeline live and
hold the clock at the playhead.
**Not in scope:** events on the timeline (J79), sound clips, several
timelines per place (one per place to start).

## Pieces
- **J57.1** The timeline strip: ruler (bars and beats), playhead, play,
  pause, scrub, tracks (a thing or a kind), clips from the library (add,
  move, stretch, delete, snap to the beat), playing live in the Studio's
  view. *Check:* Studio → Timeline: add a track on the lamps, drag "swell"
  onto it at beat 1: the lamps swell as the playhead passes
- **J57.2** The Loop Maker: tap a clip to open its pattern: lanes per
  channel, shapes, amounts, keys drawn with a finger, presets; save as a
  new pattern in the library. *Check:* open a clip, draw a new curve on its
  glow lane: the thing follows it at once
- **J57.3** Fades, mix, spread, copy, the loop region and zoom; Changes in
  plain words; publish and history. *Check:* publish; the bar on the phone
  plays the new timeline within seconds

## Done when

timeline round-trips through the draft and the store without loss.

## Risks

the width.
