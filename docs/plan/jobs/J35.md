## Goal
Every object in the bar is a real, separate thing: its own model at real
size, sitting on something real, carrying its own light and sound. You can
move any of them in the Studio, and later pick them up, knock them over or
use them (J24, J25).

## Scope
The bar, the arcade, room 3's tape and home: their models, their place files
and kinds (`assets/world/`), the asset scripts (`tools/assets/`), the place
check. **Not in scope:** new places (the street front, the city: parked),
new interactions (J24), physics props (J25), new games.

## Design
- **The kit (J35):** one rule for every new thing: a kind in
  `assets/world/kinds/`, a model from a script in `tools/assets/` at real
  size, materials from `assets/world/materials.json`, and a budget (triangles
  and texture memory per thing, checked by a tool next to
  `tools/assets/glb_report.py`). Copies of one model are drawn together
  (instancing) so fifty bottles cost about as much as one. The inventory flags
  anything still baked into a room model, and that count goes down to zero.
- **Taking apart (J70–J73):** each room model in turn; every part becomes a
  thing where it stands today (same place, same look), so the bar looks the
  same before the remake's later steps improve it. Before/after pictures per
  piece.
- **Shells (J74):** what's left of each room model is walls, floors and
  ceilings, remade at real size with real materials; doors and windows become
  things.
- **Placement (J75):** every thing sits on something real; the Studio snaps
  to it; the place check refuses floating, sunk or overlapping things.
- **Light and sound (J76, J77):** each light and sound moves from its helper
  spot onto the thing that makes it.
- **Options not taken:** remaking everything in one go (too big for a
  session, and a broken bar in between); keeping room models and only adding
  new things (the user asked for every object to be real and separate).

## Pieces
Each job is split into pieces when it starts (one model or one group of
things per piece), each with its pictures and a phone check: "stand by the
fire: the sofa, the chair and the rug look as before; in the Studio, tap the
vase and move it".

## Done when
The inventory shows no room model holding objects; every thing sits on
something; every light and sound has its thing; the bar looks at least as
good on your phone, and the frame time is no worse (J37's report).

## Risks
- **Phone speed:** many separate things mean more draw calls. Instancing and
  the budget hold it; the frame time is measured before and after each step.
- **The live world:** the Studio's store is the live world; each step is
  published to the store after its deploy, as every world change is.
- **Looks:** Claude can't see the phone; your phone check judges each step.
