## Goal
Dev feels like a place you move through, not a pile of lists: every screen
leads somewhere by tapping (projects → their tools, the plan → a chapter →
a job → its spec and pieces), a trail at the top shows where you are and
takes you back, and a search finds any tool, job or page by a word. It is
alive: the Ember glow drifts behind everything, cards and rows answer your
finger (they press in, ripple from where you touched, tilt a little), pages
slide in and out, numbers count, lights pulse. Home opens on the **Live
panel**: what's happening right now (the bar, who's in it, both deploys, the
job in Now, Claude's latest), updating by itself and animating whatever just
changed, with one-tap reactions that reach Claude (Looks good, Broke it, a
note).

## Where it stands (2026-10-25, pictures at phone width)
Five tabs of flat lists: the Plan tab is about 6,000 px tall, "Up next" is a

## Design
- **Motion (dev.css, `motion` on `<html>`):** a living background (three
  Ember glows drifting on long loops, transform only); entrance staggers;
  press, ripple from the touch point and a small tilt on every card and row;
  LEDs that breathe; meters and numbers that animate to their value; views
  slide (the View Transitions API where Safari has it, a CSS slide
  elsewhere). All of it off with Settings → Calm, or iPhone's Reduce
  Motion; everything pauses while the page is hidden.
- **Nested pages (dev.js router):** `#/projects/bar3d`, `#/plan/engine`,
  `#/plan/J85`…; a top bar with the trail (Dev › Plan › The engine › J85) and
  Back; drill-down rows; "Up next" as a row of chips you can tap; the road
  grouped by chapter, folded; Done and Parked behind their own pages.
- **Find:** one field over every tool, job, chapter and page: type a word,
  tap the result.
- **The Live panel (Home):** tiles for the bar (build), the room (people in
  the bar now, a heartbeat line that beats with them), each deploy (a
  running bar while tests run, green or red after), the job in Now (a ring
  of its pieces), Claude's latest (the newest Done line), your notes; polled
  every 15 s while open; a value that changes flashes and bumps. Each tile
  has reactions: Looks good, Broke it (a note "broke <what>" for Claude) or
  Note (a short note), sent through Dev's notes.

drifting glow sits behind it, never on top.
