# Dev: the top bar and the tab bar

*Page and controls* · joshuavelasco.com/dev (every page) · [all pages](README.md)

On every page of Dev. Dev is for the owner and the admins ([Dev](../systems/tools/dev.md)). The top bar says where you are (the trail: Dev, the tab, then each step down, like Dev › Plan › The engine › J85); every step but the last is a way back. The tab bar at the bottom changes tab; the tab you're under stays lit on the pages below it.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Back` | One step up (shows only on a page under a tab: a chapter, a job, a project, Done…). |
| `Calm` | Stops all the motion (the drifting glow, the slides, the counting, the tilt); tap again to bring it back. Remembered on this phone. On by itself if the iPhone's Reduce Motion is on. |
| `Dev` | Back to the start (Overview). |
| `Find` | Opens Find: one field over every page, project, tool, chapter and job ([Find](dev-find.md)). |
| `Overview` | Is everything up (the Live panel), what needs you, the job in Now. |
| `People` | Who may come into the bar. |
| `Plan` | The plan, and your notes to Claude. |
| `Projects` | Every project and its tools. |
| `Publish` | News, highlights and the front page's words. |

Every page has its own address (#plan/engine, #plan/J85, #projects/bar3d), so a reload, the phone's back swipe and a saved link all land on the same page. Pages slide in from the right going deeper and from the left coming back; a slide that takes longer than 1.5 s on a slow phone is dropped and the page shows at once.
