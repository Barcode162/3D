# Dev: the tab bar

*Page and controls* · joshuavelasco.com/dev (every tab) · [all pages](README.md)

The bar at the bottom of Dev, on every tab. Dev is for the owner and the admins ([Dev](../systems/tools/dev.md)).

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Overview` | Is everything up, what needs you, the job in Now. |
| `People` | Who may come into the bar. |
| `Plan` | The plan, and your notes to Claude. |
| `Projects` | Every project and its tools. |
| `Publish` | News, highlights and the front page's words. |
