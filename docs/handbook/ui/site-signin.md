# The site: Sign in

*Page and controls* · joshuavelasco.com/signin · [all pages](README.md)

Sign in, or make an account (13 and up).

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Come in as a guest` | Into the bar without an account (if the door lets guests in). |
| `Home` | Back to the front page. |
| `New account` | Switch to making an account (asks for a birth date). |
| `Sign in` | Sign in with your username and password. |
| `the door` | Into the bar's door (where you sign in for the bar). |
