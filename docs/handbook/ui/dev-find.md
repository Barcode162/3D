# Dev → Find

*Page and controls* · the magnifier in Dev's top bar · [all pages](README.md)

One field over everything Dev knows: every page, every project and tool, every chapter and job on the plan (by its number, its old number or any word of it). The answers show as you type, the best first; tap one, or press Enter for the first. Empty, it lists the pages and the projects.

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| `Find` | Where you type. |
