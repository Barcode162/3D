# The Bar3D manual

Plain-words pages for everything in the bar and the Studio: what it is,
where to find it, and its dials. Each piece of the plan (`PLAN.md`)
writes its section here when it's built.

- [Light](light.md): How light works in the bar: where it comes from, how it spreads, and every dial for it in the Studio.
- [Materials](materials.md): What surfaces are made of and how they catch light: textures, wear, dirt, layers, light passing through.
- [Timeline and script](timeline.md): The bar script language, the timeline at the centre of the Studio, and Launch.
- [Recipes and modelling](recipes.md): Models grown from recipes, Claude building live in the Studio, scanning real things.
- [Avatars](avatars.md): People in the bar: how they move, look and feel alive.
- [4K Mania](4k-mania.md): How the rhythm game keeps time, the sync, the stars, and how the Training maps are built: every pattern and the rules each map keeps.
- [World axes](world.md): Dials beyond left/right, up/down, forward/back: memory, mood, chance, time, era, scale, layers.
