# Bartending

*System page · chapter: world* · [all systems](../../generated/systems.md)

## What it is

Shifts behind the bar for players with the bartender badge: make drinks step by step, put them on the counter for anyone.

## Status

**Live.**

## What you can do with it

With the badge: start a shift at the station, pick a drink (Menu → the drink list), make it (glass, ice, pour, shake, garnish), each step with its sound; anyone can take the finished drink and sip.

## How it works

`bar/bartender.*`; the badge is given by the owner (`server/account.php` badges).

## Data

The badge on the account; finished drinks in the shared world.

## Commands

`?shift`.

## Limits

Crude animations (marked for a remaster).

## Checks

The phone.

## Known traps

Only owners give badges.
