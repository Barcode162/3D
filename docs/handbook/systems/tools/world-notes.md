# World notes

*System page · chapter: tools* · [all systems](../../generated/systems.md)

## What it is

Every kind of thing in the bar in plain words: its states and rules, how many there are (joshuavelasco.com/hub/world.html).

## Status

**Live.**

## What you can do with it

Read what each kind does; filter Alive / Everything; each kind links to its Dials.

## How it works

`hub/world.html` reads the bar's kind and place files and writes them out in words.

## Data

`assets/world/kinds/bar.json`, `places/bar.json` (the built-in copy on the site).
