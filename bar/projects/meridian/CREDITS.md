# Meridian credits

Meridian is the user's own driving game (made 2026-10, brought into the bar
2026-10-18). Its Blender build scripts are in `incoming/meridian/blender/`.

- **three.js 0.169** (MIT license), loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net: the only
  outside engine in the project, used by this game only (the bar itself is
  our own engine). Approved by the user on 2026-10-18 ("then add it").
- **Textures and nature scans** (rocks, shrubs, quiver tree, road surfaces):
  Poly Haven, CC0 (public domain), packed by `blender/pack_textures.py` and
  `blender/nature.py`.
- **Fonts:** Instrument Serif (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License) and
  Satoshi (Fontshare, free for personal and commercial use).
- Car, props and road: modelled by the game's own scripts.
